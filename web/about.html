About us

Our purpose

Helping students find a place to focus

QuietSpot helps students find a suitable place to study based on their needs. It shows nearby study spots with their location, crowd level, noise level, open seats, facilities, and price, and lets students share what a spot is like right now so others can decide where to go.

Our core features

1

Search and filter

Filter by noise level, crowd level, facilities, and price, and sort by distance.

2

Live spot status

See estimated open seats, crowd, and noise, plus the usual busy hours of each spot.

3

Community updates

Students report conditions, add new spots, and upvote or downvote reports to keep them accurate.

4

Favorites and rewards

Save favorite spots, get directions, and earn points and badges for helpful updates.

Project limitations

  • Only spots in Alangilan, Batangas City that are added or registered in the system are covered.
  • Seats, crowd, and noise depend on user updates and may not always be accurate.
  • No sensors or cameras, no real Wi-Fi speed testing, no payments or booking.
  • Rewards are basic points and badges only.
